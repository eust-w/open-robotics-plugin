# Collision-Aware Motion Planning ([`motion_planner.py`](scripts/motion_planner.py))

Import `import motion_planner as mp` (prepending `<skill_dir>/scripts` to `sys.path` if needed).
> **CRITICAL — Never `cat`, `head`, or `tail` `motion_planner.py` or raw robot XMLs!** Check `README.md`/runtime specs for transport, action layout, `control_dt_s`, and camera intrinsics, write workspace `.py` scripts or `python3 - << 'EOF'` (avoid `/tmp` scripts or inline `python3 -c` quoting errors), and call `mjcf_path = mp.resolve_mjcf_path()`, `print(mp.describe_robot(mjcf_path)["summary_text"])`, and `planner.gripper_frame_info(gripper_cmd)` upfront for joints/limits, TCP axes (`approach_axis_ee`, `closing_axis_ee`, `slim_axis_ee`), jaw widths, and `asymmetric_protrusions`.

## 1. Calibration, Hollow Geometry & Immediate `t = 0` Escape
- **MJCF Root Frame & Sparse Triangulation**: Express all poses/primitives in the **robot MJCF root frame** (never assume `z_support = 0.0` or mix external world extrinsics). Compute camera poses via `mp.get_camera_poses_in_robot_frame(mjcf_path, joint_positions)` (`{"left": left_q, "right": right_q}`, `{joint_name: val}`, or single-arm `q`) and triangulate 1–2 support points (`z_support`, `max_ray_residual_m < 0.015`) plus sparse landmarks across `>= 2` views via `mp.triangulate_pixels` (standing height `h = z_top - z_support`, resting center `z = z_support + 0.5 * h`, or `mp.intersect_ray_plane` — never run dense stereo, RANSAC, or `scipy.optimize` silhouette fitters).
- **Safety-Margin Overlay Fit (`~1–1.5 cm`), Hollow Primitives & Door Padding**: Persist `scene_model.py` (`build_scene`, `build_object`). Decompose hollow bowls, cups, racks, and cavities into **thin wall, floor/sill, ceiling, and rim primitives** (`add_vertical_cylinder` base + perimeter ring of vertical `add_capsule` walls for round vessels, or `add_oriented_box` panels for box cavities — never solid blocks sealing interior goals). Model support (`group="table"`), ungrasped target (`group="target_init"`, removed via `scene.without("target_init")` once attached), and idle arms (`planner.add_other_arm_to_scene`). Pad fixed walls `3–5 mm` and **unlatched hinged doors `~18–25 mm` (`group="door"`)** (`>= 12–15 cm` staging clearance). **Calibrate to the safety margin (`~1–1.5 cm`), not subpixel perfection**: once wireframes in `mp.render_scene_overlay` enclose obstacles/rims within `~1–1.5 cm` (`2–3` passes max), **stop refining geometry and move to execution** (avoid custom Canny, vanishing-point, or numerical solvers; use close-range wrist views at `pre_grasp`/`pre_place` if needed).
- **Immediate All-Arm `t = 0` Escape**: Check **every arm** at `t = 0` via `planner.check_arm_config(scene, q_init)`. If any arm has `min_clearance_m < 0.025`, **execute `planner.escape_initial_collision(scene, q_init, target_clearance_m=0.04)["q_trajectory"]` right away before planning pick/place**, re-observe, update the arm's parked pose, and only re-fit `scene_model.py` if an object shifted.

## 2. Motion Planning, Lookahead Feasibility & Checkpoint Execution
- **Free-Space `plan_arm` vs. Linear `plan_cartesian_stroke`**: Use `planner.plan_arm` (`safety_margin_m=0.015`) between pre-contact poses (`5–8 cm` clear), attaching held objects via `planner.attach_object_at_world_pose(obj, q_grasp, obj_pose6d)` and `planner.set_gripper_opening(gripper_cmd)`. **Always use `planner.plan_cartesian_stroke(..., max_joint_vel_rad_s=0.5)`** (never 2-point joint interpolation) for `pre_grasp -> grasp`, vertical lift clear of rims, `pre_place -> place -> retract`, and linear cavity insertions/pushes (`ignore_obstacles=("table",)` or `clearance_tol_m=0.003` at contact). Hold targets **`~0.4–0.5 s`** when closing or fully opening (`0.0`) the gripper before lifting/retracting. Center grasps on `ee_site`, align `closing_axis_ee` across the wider clearance axis so `slim_axis_ee` faces narrow jambs/neighbors (or along thin rims/rods), and flip wrist roll `180°` (`ee_rot @ np.diag([-1, -1, 1])`) so `asymmetric_protrusions` face open space.
- **Single-Arm Reach First & Decoupled Regrasp Staging**: First check whether **a single arm can both pick and reach a valid goal feature directly**. When reorientation or cross-workspace transfer is required, **prefer placing stably on an unobstructed surface (clear of open doors/obstacles), releasing, re-observing, and regrasping** to decouple the arms at a zero-risk checkpoint — reserve mid-air bimanual handover strictly for slender/non-standing objects that cannot rest stably on a surface, and **if coupled two-arm or multi-stage planning fails once or twice offline, switch immediately to surface staging or an alternate reachable goal feature rather than tuning coupled IK in a loop**.
- **Lookahead Endpoint Feasibility, Then Checkpoint-by-Checkpoint Execution**: Reserve **`>= 75%` of episode budget** for execution. Before grasping, run a fast **endpoint feasibility check** (`solve_ik` + `check_arm_config` / `check_se3_pose` at grasp and goal placement) to avoid kinematic dead ends — **but only synthesize `plan_arm` / `plan_cartesian_stroke` trajectories up to the next safe, reversible physical checkpoint** (collision escape, lifted hover, surface staging, or single-item placement), then **execute right away and re-observe real camera + joint feedback** rather than pre-solving multi-stage trajectories or all `N` items offline. After each placement, update `scene` with placed items at their observed resting poses and triangulate 3D position to verify it lies deep inside the goal volume (following shallow doorway-sill releases with a closed-gripper linear centerline push, or using a secure upper support feature on tall stands), parking arms `>= 10 cm` clear when done.

## 3. `motion_planner` (`mp`) API (`pose6d = [x, y, z, yaw, pitch, roll]`)
- **Introspection & SE(3)**: `mp.resolve_mjcf_path() -> str`, `mp.describe_robot(mjcf_path=None) -> {"arms", "cameras", "summary_text"}`, `mp.rpy_to_matrix(r, p, y)`, `mp.matrix_to_rpy(rot)`, `mp.quat_wxyz_to_matrix(q_wxyz)`, `mp.pose6d_from_pos_rot(pos, rot=None)`, `mp.pos_rot_from_pose6d(pose6d) -> (pos, rot)`, `mp.make_transform(pos, rot=None)`, `mp.invert_transform(tf)`.
- **`Scene3D` & `MultiSphereObject`**: `scene = mp.Scene3D()`: `.add_sphere(name, center, radius, group="")`, `.add_aligned_box(name, center, half_extents, group="")`, `.add_oriented_box(name, center, half_extents, rotation, group="")`, `.add_capsule(name, point_a, point_b, radius, group="")`, `.add_vertical_cylinder(name, center, radius, height, group="")`, `.add_object_spheres(obj, pose6d, name_prefix="", group="")`, `.without(*names_or_groups)`, `.clone()`. `obj = mp.MultiSphereObject(name="object")`: `.add_sphere(local_center, radius)`, `.add_capsule_spheres(p0, p1, radius, num_spheres=5)`. `mp.check_se3_pose(obj, scene, pose6d) -> {"valid", "collisions", "min_clearance_m"}`, `mp.plan_object_se3(obj, scene, start_pose6d, goal_pose6d, safety_margin_m=0.010) -> {"solved", "waypoints", "interpolated"}`.
- **`RobotArmPlanner`**: `planner = mp.RobotArmPlanner(mjcf_path=None, arm="left", gripper_cmd=0.0)` (`.dof`, `.q_min`, `.q_max`, `.joint_names`, `.ee_site_name`): `.fk(q) -> (ee_pos, ee_rot)`, `.gripper_frame_info(gripper_cmd=None) -> {"approach_axis_ee", "closing_axis_ee", "slim_axis_ee", "open_inner_gap_m", "closed_inner_gap_m", "open_outer_width_m", "slim_profile_width_m", "asymmetric_protrusions", "top_down_rot_hint"}`, `.set_gripper_opening(gripper_cmd)`, `.attach_object_at_world_pose(obj, q_grasp, obj_world_pose6d) -> T_ee_obj`, `.ee_pose_for_object_pose(obj_world_pose6d) -> (ee_pos, ee_rot)`, `.attached_object_pose(q) -> pose6d`, `.detach_object()`, `.add_other_arm_to_scene(scene, other_arm_q, other_gripper_cmd=0.0)`, `.check_arm_config(scene, q, ignore_obstacles=(), clearance_tol_m=0.0) -> {"valid", "self_collisions", "collisions", "min_clearance_m", "nearest_pair"}`, `.solve_ik(target_pos, target_rot=None, q_seed=None, scene=None, num_restarts=16, ignore_obstacles=(), clearance_tol_m=0.0) -> {"success", "q", "pos_err_m", "rot_err_rad", "collision_check"}`, `.escape_initial_collision(scene, q_start, target_clearance_m=0.015, control_dt_s=0.04) -> {"escaped", "q_escaped", "q_trajectory"}`, `.plan_cartesian_stroke(q_start, target_pos, target_rot=None, num_waypoints=8, scene=None, ignore_obstacles=(), clearance_tol_m=0.0, max_joint_vel_rad_s=0.55, control_dt_s=0.04) -> {"solved", "waypoints", "q_trajectory", "min_clearance_m"}`, `.plan_arm(scene, q_start, q_goal, max_joint_vel_rad_s=1.25, control_dt_s=0.04, safety_margin_m=0.015) -> {"solved", "waypoints", "q_trajectory"}`, `mp.resample_waypoints_cosine(waypoints, max_joint_vel_rad_s=1.25, control_dt_s=0.04) -> np.ndarray`.
- **Cameras & Overlay**: `mp.get_camera_poses_in_robot_frame(mjcf_path, joint_positions=None) -> {cam_key: {"pos", "rot", "convention", "mjcf_name"}}` (MuJoCo `-Z` forward for `mp.*`), `mp.get_camera_poses_opencv(mjcf_path, joint_positions=None, image_size=None) -> {cam_key: {"pos", "rot", "convention", "T_world_cam", "T_ee_cam", "K", "fx_px", "fy_px", "cx_px", "cy_px", "arm", "ee_site_name", "mjcf_name"}}` (OpenCV `+Z` forward for `rgb_localization` / `Ibvs`; pass `image_size=(W, H)` when MJCF omits `resolution`), `mp.triangulate_pixels(uv_by_camera, cam_poses, cam_intrinsics) -> {"point_3d", "max_ray_residual_m"}`, `mp.intersect_ray_plane(u_col, v_row, cam_pose, cam_intrinsics, plane_z=0.0)`, `mp.render_scene_overlay(mjcf_path, joint_positions, camera_image_paths, scene, output_path, objects=(), camera_intrinsics=None) -> str`.
