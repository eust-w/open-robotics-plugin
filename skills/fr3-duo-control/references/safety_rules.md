# Franka Research 3 Duo Safety Rules & Operational Rationale (`R1–R16`)

Always follow `R1–R16` when operating, programming, or bringing up a dual-arm **FR3 Duo** (`left`/`right` 7-DOF FR3 arms + Robotiq 2F-85 grippers + ZED Mini + RealSense D405 cameras).

## Summary of Rules `R1–R16`

| Rule | Title | Technical Enforcement & Operational Gate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R1** | Confirm Desk & get `"go"` before live motion | Confirm Desk brakes open, FCI enabled, tilted Duo gravity vector + Robotiq 2F-85/wrist-camera payload configured. Before energizing live actuators, state arms, start/end poses, `z_min`, peak joint speed, and abort instructions, and wait for explicit human `"go"`. Never ask `"go"` for `--dry-run`, `--read-only`, camera checks, or software setup. |
| **R2** | Auto-run `--dry-run` first | Always run `--dry-run` (offline `Fr3DuoSafetyChecker` bounds, collision, and FK validation with zero hardware access) before requesting live `"go"`. **Once `config/robot_config.json` is populated and hardware is verified**, `--dry-run` the target task directly without re-running bring-up motion checks. For first-time bring-up, step `env -> preflight -> cameras -> gripper -> nudge -> abort-test -> hello-world`, changing one variable per trial. |
| **R3** | Abort key on every motion (`q` + `Enter`) | `AbortWatcher` monitors stdin (`q` + `Enter`) and `SIGINT` (`Ctrl-C`), smoothly decelerating ($C^2$) and unwinding backwards along `travelled_waypoints` to `rest_pose` **without opening grippers** so held payloads are never dropped mid-air. |
| **R4** | Clean FCI stop & post-E-stop recovery | Never close a 1 kHz `pylibfranka` control loop mid-motion; decelerate to zero velocity/acceleration (`MotionFinished`) at `rest_pose` first. After any E-stop or Franka reflex, clear errors in Desk, restart FCI + Robotiq serial sessions, and re-run `bringup.py --step preflight` and `--step gripper` before resuming motion. |
| **R5** | Speed, $C^2$ quintic, joint & clearance limits | Use $C^2$ quintics (`<= 15 deg/s` under `kIgnore`, `<= 30 deg/s` on `PREEMPT_RT` `kEnforce`, `<= 45 deg/s` explicit `--realtime enforce`), `0.100 rad` J4 margin (`[-2.9421, -0.2518] rad`), `<= 7.5 rad/s^2` / `3500 rad/s^3` on J1–J2 (`9.5` / `4500` on J3–J7), `>= 0.030 m` inter-arm clearance, `z_table_m` tabletop floor, and live `q_rad` re-reads across skill/gripper boundaries. |
| **R6** | Pre-flight cameras & free-air `tau_ext` | Verify all 4 canonical RGB streams (`age <= 0.30 s`, non-frozen digests) and free-air `tau_ext <= 6.5 N` (`tau_ext_hat_filtered <= 4.5 Nm`) before motion. Joint torque sensors under-read downward (`-Z`) contact by `~2.8x`: cap `tau_ext <= 9.0 N` (probing) and `<= 18.0 N` (manipulation). |
| **R7** | Record video of every motion | Record continuous 4-camera MP4 + telemetry from before leaving `rest_pose` until returning cleanly to `rest_pose`. |
| **R8** | Forward kinematics (`fr3_kinematics.py`) | Verify `fk_pos_error_m <= 5.0 mm` (`0.005 m`) between `fr3_kinematics.compute_arm_pinch_from_flange` and Desk `O_T_EE` (`F_T_EE`-compensated) before motion. |
| **R9** | Honest grasp & contact verification | Never bypass tactile checks with geometry shortcuts (like `or tcp_dz <= ...`) or infer grasps from width alone; verify jaw travel shortfall (`RobotiqStatus.obj_status` / `gOBJ == 2`, `position_ratio < 0.92`) corroborated by load delta or multi-view vision. |
| **R10** | Re-verify after drift or bumps | Re-run `--step preflight` (`discover_hardware.py`) and camera checks if arms, cameras, or tabletop shift. |
| **R11** | Persistent `tmux` session `robot` | Run interactive hardware CLIs and camera/control scripts inside `tmux` session `robot` so the operator can monitor telemetry and issue `q` + `Enter` aborts. |
| **R12** | Keep `NOTES.md` & `robot_config.json` current | Read `NOTES.md` before each session and update `NOTES.md` and `config/robot_config.json` after calibration or hardware changes. |
| **R13** | Clean single-purpose scripts | Write self-checking scripts with `--dry-run` and JSON config; keep `--dry-run` tests green before hardware runs. |
| **R14** | 6-field stage report | Report `Result`, `Evidence`, `Numbers measured`, `Done-when checks`, `Concerns`, and `Need from you` after bring-up or task runs. |
| **R15** | Auto-advance non-motion stages | Auto-advance across software, `--dry-run`, and read-only checks; pause only for physical human actions or `R1` live-motion `"go"`. |
| **R16** | Human safety & E-stop first | Keep the physical E-stop within immediate operator reach and ensure actuators are idle before any human touches the arms, cables, or workspace. |

## 1. Pre-Flight & Franka Desk Configuration (`R1`, `R2`, `R6`, `R8`)

*   **Tilted Duo Gravity & EE Payload in Franka Desk (`R1`, `R6`):** On angled Duo pedestals, each arm's `link0` `+Z` axis is tilted away from world vertical. If Franka Desk is left at default upright `(0, 0, -9.81)` gravity or `0 kg` end-effector mass (ignoring the `~0.95 kg` Robotiq 2F-85 + coupling + wrist RealSense), gravity compensation is wrong before motion starts—causing `> 15 N` free-air `tau_ext` bias and sudden downward droop when FCI engages. `fr3_arm_controller.audit_arm_desk_config` and `discover_hardware.run_discovery` verify `fk_pos_error_m <= 5.0 mm` (`R8`) and free-air `tau_ext <= 6.5 N` (`R6`).
*   **Structural `--dry-run` Interlock (`R2`):** Passing `--dry-run` runs offline MuJoCo trajectory & FK collision previews via `fr3_safety.Fr3DuoSafetyChecker` without connecting to hardware/grippers, structurally preventing physical actuation. Separately, `read_only=True` with `mode="hardware", dry_run=False` connects to live FCI arms/cameras for read-only observation while blocking all motion/recovery commands.
*   **4-Camera Freshness & Non-Frozen Digest (`R6`):** Cached or USB-stalled camera buffers can report advancing host timestamps while delivering identical pixels. `fr3_camera_controller.Fr3DuoCameraController.check_freshness` verifies both frame age (`<= 0.30 s`) and pixel variation across repeated reads.

## 2. 1 kHz FCI Streaming, Smooth $C^2$ Trajectories & Safe Abort (`R3`, `R4`, `R5`)

*   **Never Drop an Active 1 kHz FCI Loop Mid-Motion (`R4`):** Abruptly closing a `pylibfranka` control session while joint velocity or acceleration is nonzero triggers an immediate `communication_constraints_violation` / mechanical brake reflex, jarring gears and risking dropped payloads. Every trajectory uses $C^2$-continuous quintic polynomials (`v = 0, a = 0` at start and end) via `fr3_safety.generate_quintic_trajectory` and finishes with `MotionFinished` only after decelerating to rest.
*   **Process Isolation & Real-Time Scaling (`R5`):**
    *   **1 kHz loop isolation:** Each arm's FCI loop runs in an isolated `ArmWorkerProcess` (`fr3_arm_controller.py`) with Python GC paused during motion so GIL/GC pauses never miss the 1 ms deadline.
    *   **Speed tiers:**
        *   Non-`PREEMPT_RT` (`--realtime auto` -> `RealtimeConfig.kIgnore`): `<= 15 deg/s` bring-up default (tunable via `max_joint_speed_deg_s` up to `45 deg/s` in `config/robot_config.json`).
        *   `PREEMPT_RT` (`--realtime auto` -> `RealtimeConfig.kEnforce`): `<= 30 deg/s` bring-up cap.
        *   Explicit `--realtime enforce`: unlocks up to `<= 45 deg/s`.
*   **Reverse Path Unwind Without Mid-Air Release (`R3`):** When `fr3_safety.AbortWatcher` catches `q` + `Enter` or `SIGINT`, the active trajectory decelerates smoothly (`C^2` stop) and unwinds backwards along `travelled_waypoints` to `rest_pose` while **preserving the current Robotiq gripper command**.
*   **Workspace Floor & Bimanual Clearance (`R5`):** Fit a 2D tilted tabletop plane `z_at_xy(x, y)` when table pitch is nonzero, clamp `fr3_joint4` with a `0.100 rad` margin (`[-2.9421, -0.2518] rad`), cap J1–J2 acceleration at `<= 7.5 rad/s^2`, and maintain `>= 0.030 m` inter-arm clearance (`RobotArmPlanner.add_other_arm_to_scene` + `Fr3DuoSafetyChecker`). Normal tasks keep `allow_initial_table_lift=False` (strict `z_table_m` floor); `--step home` passes `allow_initial_table_lift=True` to permit upward-only lift-off from an initial tabletop contact (`<= 0.015 m`).
*   **Re-Read Live `q_rad` Across Skill & Gripper Boundaries (`R5`):** Always re-read `q_now = robot.read_state()[arm]["q_rad"]` when transitioning between skill boundaries (e.g., `visual-servoing` / `ibvs` -> `motion-planning`) or between arm and gripper commands (`move_gripper()` -> `move_arm_waypoints` / `move_bimanual_waypoints` / `plan_cartesian_stroke`). Opening/closing the gripper or external contact near the table can deflect the arm, and `Fr3DuoRobot` prepends current `q_start` whenever `waypoints[0]` differs by `> 1e-4 rad`; reusing a cached pre-gripper `q_at_grasp` as `waypoints[0]` can command an unintended plunge into the table and trip `cartesian_reflex`.

## 3. External Force/Torque (`tau_ext`) Estimation Without a Wrist FT Sensor (`R6`, `R9`)

*   **Coupled `6x7` Spatial Jacobian SVD:** Because standard FR3 Duo cells have no wrist FT-300 force-torque sensor, external 6D wrenches in `base` frame (`+w_env_on_robot`) are estimated from joint torque residuals (`tau_ext`) via `mj_rne` (`qvel = 0`) and a coupled `6x7` spatial Jacobian SVD (`s_floor = 0.05`) in `fr3_kinematics.estimate_external_wrench_base`—never split `3x3` blocks, which leak wrist moments into phantom linear forces.
*   **Vertical `-Z` `~2.8x` Under-Read Warning (`R6`):** Arm-Jacobian `tau_ext` under-reads vertical downward (`-Z`) tip contact relative to physical table force by roughly `2.8x` (`~13.7 N` `tau_ext` corresponds to `~38.4 N` physical force), with `3-4 N` free-air posture drift. Set guarded tabletop contact thresholds `>= 4.5 N` after local free-air tare, enforce `tau_ext` ceilings of **`<= 9.0 N` (~25 N physical) during tabletop probing** and **`<= 18.0 N` (~50 N physical) during manipulation**, and hit the E-stop immediately on visible wrist bowing or pad deflection.
*   **Honest Grasp & Contact Verification (`R9`):** Never bypass tactile contact checks with geometry shortcuts (like `or tcp_dz <= ...`), and never verify Robotiq grasps from width alone (`empty` closure stalls at `0.82-0.91` with `~26 N` internal pad force). Confirm nonzero jaw travel plus `obj_status` (`gOBJ == 2` property alias on `RobotiqStatus`) stall shortfall (`< 0.92`) corroborated by load delta or multi-view camera checks.
